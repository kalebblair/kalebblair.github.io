Scarlet Sacrifice

Scarlet Sacrifice is 2D action platformer where players consume their own health to traverse and attack. It was a solo project for GAME 310 where I was tasked to create a game with at least 5 minutes of gameplay. I had to document, design, program, and implement everything within a 7 week time-span. Usage of outside assets was allowed.

Cybersin

Cybersin is a top down, soft bullet hell, with a sci-fi theme and an 8-bit style, built for PC. Slash and blast through enemies as you clear through the levels and move on to the next. A group project for GAME 310 consisting of 5 members with the requirement of at least 15 minutes of gameplay. My role on the team was one of the two programmers, but I did help with some of the design choices. I mainly focused on the player, player weaponry, UI, power-up functionality and spawning, & other miscellaneous tasks. Usage of outside assets was allowed.

Aw Sap!

Aw Sap! is a 3D platformer where you play as a squirrel attempting to escape the ever enclosing tree sap. It was made in 48-hours for IGDA DC's District Jam #7 with a team size of 9, in which I served as a generalist. I worked on basic 3D models seen throughout the level, I made the second level, & did VFX implementation.

Poised

Poised is an endless 2D side-scroller with a grid inventory upgrade system. It was a group project for GAME 399 utilizing the Cursor AI Agent in Godot. Cursor was mainly utilized for programming work as it suffered greatly when working with visuals. I served as the main designer on the project coming up with the original concept, balancing the game feel, & implementing visuals (except UI).
